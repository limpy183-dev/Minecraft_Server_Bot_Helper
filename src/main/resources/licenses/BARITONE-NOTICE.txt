To replace the dependency, substitute a compatible Baritone Fabric jar in
libs, update the dependency coordinates and checksum in build.gradle and
the Baritone dependency version in fabric.mod.json, then run gradlew build.
An external compatible Baritone mod can also satisfy Fabric's dependency.
No restriction is imposed on debugging modifications to the included library.

Baritone also bundles nether-pathfinder 1.6. Its original notices and license
remain within that dependency's jar, nested inside the Baritone jar.
